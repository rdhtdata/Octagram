Bakery Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Common questions bakery owners ask when digitizing their operations.

How do customers place custom cake orders on the website?

Customers use an interactive cake inquiry form where they pick size, sponge flavour, frosting, theme, message, date of occasion, and upload a design reference. The full spec is dispatched directly to your kitchen.

Can we specify delivery radius and time slots?

Yes. You can limit delivery to specific postal codes/neighborhoods and enforce order cutoff times (e.g. "Order 24 hours in advance for custom bakes").

Can we accept direct payments?

Yes. We integrate Razorpay, UPI QR codes, credit cards, and Apple/Google Pay for instant cashless settlements without aggregator markups.

Is the site optimized for local searches like "best bakery near me"?

Yes. We implement comprehensive LocalBusiness Bakery schema and local keyword architecture to capture local cake and pastry searches in your city.
