Café Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Common questions cafe founders ask when upgrading their digital storefront.

Can we sell packaged coffee beans on our cafe website?

Yes. We can integrate a lightweight e-commerce storefront allowing customers to purchase freshly roasted bags with grind selection (Whole Bean, French Press, Aeropress, Espresso) and ship anywhere.

How does WhatsApp pickup ordering work?

Customers tap the items they want on your menu. When they hit "Order on WhatsApp", an auto-formatted message with their order breakdown and pickup time is sent directly to your barista phone.

Can the site show our daily rotational brew list?

Yes. We provide an easy-to-update structure so your team can change the featured single origin beans and daily pastries in under two minutes.

Is the website optimized for neighbourhood discovery?

Yes. We embed structured LocalBusiness JSON-LD schema, geotargeted keywords, and direct Google Maps pins so your cafe ranks at the top when locals search for "specialty coffee near me".
