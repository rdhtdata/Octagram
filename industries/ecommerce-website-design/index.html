E-commerce Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Key insights for merchants looking to launch or scale their online storefront.

Can we migrate products from Shopify or WooCommerce?

Yes. We provide full CSV and API data migration tools that transfer your product catalog, variant matrices, customer records, and order history without downtime.

How do you handle shipping rate calculations?

We integrate real-time courier APIs (Shiprocket, Delhivery, FedEx, DHL) to compute automated shipping rates by weight, pincode, and delivery speed.

Can we implement discounts, coupons, and bundle offers?

Yes. You can create percentage discounts, fixed coupons, tiered volume pricing (Buy 2 Get 1 Free), and automatic cart discounts with expiry rules.

Is the checkout optimized to reduce cart abandonment?

Yes. Our single-page checkout removes unnecessary fields, auto-fills pincode city/state, remembers repeat customer addresses, and triggers automated WhatsApp recovery reminders.
