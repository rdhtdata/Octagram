Hospitality Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Common questions hotel owners ask us when planning their website development.

Can the website integrate with our existing PMS software?

Yes. We integrate directly with leading Property Management Systems including eZee Absolute, Hotelogix, Cloudbeds, and Sirvoy to synchronize real-time room availability and rates without manual updates.

How does the website help reduce OTA commissions?

By delivering a lightning-fast booking engine with instant payment, exclusive direct booking perks (e.g. complimentary breakfast badges), and zero OTA middleman markups.

Is the hotel website optimized for mobile travelers?

Over 70% of hospitality bookings happen on mobile. We build mobile-first with sticky room reservation bars, tap-to-call, tap-to-WhatsApp, and instant GPS navigation links.

Can we manage corporate & banquet enquiries?

Yes. We include custom inquiry forms for corporate block bookings, wedding banquets, and conference halls with instant email & WhatsApp alerts to your sales team.

Like what you see for your hotel?

Your property doesn't need to look like every other generic booking template. We turn this design direction into a high-converting platform customized for your rooms, brand, and guests.
