Jewellery Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Everything fine jewellery houses need to know when taking their collections online.

Can we integrate live gold & bullion rate calculators?

Yes. We can integrate dynamic daily gold/silver price feeds that automatically adjust making charges and metal weights across your inventory.

Can we display collections without showing prices?

Yes. Many high-end ateliers prefer a "Price on Request" or "Request Private Viewing" CTA for exclusive pieces, initiating a personalized WhatsApp dialogue.

How does the website handle insured shipping logistics?

We integrate secure carrier APIs (BlueDart Secure, Sequel, Brink's) with tamper-evident tracking and mandatory OTP verification upon delivery.

Can we host virtual video consultations?

Yes. We integrate one-click Google Meet and WhatsApp Video consultation booking directly into product pages for remote bridal jewelry trials.
