Real Estate Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Key questions developers and real estate brokerages ask when building their portals.

Can the site automatically route buyer leads to our CRM?

Yes. Every form submission, brochure download, and WhatsApp inquiry triggers instant webhooks into LeadSquared, Salesforce, HubSpot, or Zoho CRM with UTM campaign tags intact.

Can we display RERA certification details prominently?

Yes. We create compliant RERA verification badges, registration numbers, and official document viewer links directly in the footer and project overview blocks.

Can we embed virtual 3D walkthroughs (Matterport)?

Yes. We integrate lightweight, lazy-loaded 360° virtual tours from Matterport, Kuula, and YouTube video walkthroughs without impacting page load speed.

How does the site perform on high-intent Google Ads campaigns?

Because our pages load in under 1 second and have high Quality Scores, your Google Ads CPC drops significantly and conversion rates on landing pages improve.
