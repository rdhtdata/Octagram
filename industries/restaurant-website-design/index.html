Restaurant Inquiries

Frequently asked questions.

Everything restaurateurs need to know about building a high-impact digital presence.

Why should we replace our PDF menu with an HTML menu?

PDF menus are notoriously difficult to read on smartphones, require large data downloads, and cannot be indexed by Google. Our responsive HTML menus load instantly and boost your search rankings for specific dishes.

How easy is it to update daily specials or pricing?

We structure your menu cleanly so price adjustments, seasonal items, and new culinary creations can be modified in minutes without breaking page layouts.

Can we link our Instagram feed directly to the site?

Yes. We can integrate an automated, privacy-friendly live Instagram grid showcasing your latest dish presentations and customer tagged stories.

Can we accept direct takeaway orders?

Yes. We can integrate a direct WhatsApp ordering workflow or a lightweight pickup cart to help you bypass 30% third-party aggregator commissions.
