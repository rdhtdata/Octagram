Salon Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Common questions salon owners ask when modernizing their booking operations.

Can the booking system integrate with our salon management software?

Yes. We integrate with leading salon platforms such as Fresha, Zenoti, Vagaro, Mindbody, and Timely so your appointments sync automatically with your in-salon desk.

Can we take booking deposits to prevent no-shows?

Yes. You can require a partial or full advance deposit for high-ticket services (like Balayage, Extensions, or Bridal makeovers) through Razorpay or Stripe.

Can we sell professional retail haircare and skincare products?

Yes. We can incorporate an e-boutique to sell salon-exclusive shampoos, serums, and styling tools for clients to order online or click-and-collect.

How does the site help rank in local neighbourhood searches?

We configure rich LocalBusiness BeautySalon schema with geocoordinates, opening hours, accepted payment modes, and localized keywords for every treatment you offer.
