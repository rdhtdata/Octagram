THE INDUSTRIAL CHALLENGE

Structuring Complex B2B Product Hierarchies

Chemical distribution involves thousands of technical SKUs, strict compliance standards (COA & MSDS), and customized B2B quotation workflows.

Project Overview

Founded by industry veterans Mr. Aivanth N. Ranka and Rushika Mehta, Ranka Chemical operates as an authorized stockist for SD Fine Chem. They supply high-purity chemicals and laboratory hardware to pharmaceutical formulation plants, R&D laboratories, manufacturing units, and universities across Southern India.

The digital challenge was to establish a high-trust, compliant B2B portal that communicates technical authority, batch traceability, and direct factory procurement pricing.

The Information Architecture Challenge

Industrial website design requires structured cataloging so procurement managers and research scientists can rapidly find specific purity grades:

  • Organize thousands of compounds into 5 distinct product pillars without cognitive overload.
  • Provide seamless Request for Quotation (RFQ) inquiry cart tools tailored to volume B2B purchasing.
  • Emphasize regulatory compliance: manufacturer batch COA & MSDS traceability, temperature-controlled Bengaluru storage, and direct brand warranties.
PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE

The 5 Core Catalogue Pillars

Octagram architected a modular catalogue structure allowing procurement managers to navigate specific chemical grades, packaging formats, and laboratory hardware.

PILLAR 01

Laboratory Chemicals & Reagents

Full range of Analytical Reagents (AR/GR Grade), LR Grade synthesis chemicals, Biochemicals for life sciences, and high-purity Deuterated Solvents for NMR and spectroscopy analysis.

PILLAR 02

Pharmaceutical Raw Materials

Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs), Pharmacopoeia-grade excipients (conforming to IP, BP, USP, EP standards), chemical intermediates, and ultra-pure HPLC/ULS-MS solvents.

PILLAR 03

Industrial & Bulk Chemicals

High-volume industrial solvents (IPA, Acetone, MIBK, Toluene) and bulk acids & bases (Hydrochloric, Sulfuric, Nitric, Caustic Soda) packaged in carboys, drums, and tankers.

PILLAR 04

Labware & Glassware

Class A and Class B certified volumetric glassware with calibration certificates, high-strength borosilicate labware, interchangeable joint setups, and autoclavable polypropylene plasticware.

PILLAR 05

Filter Paper & Separation Media

Qualitative and ashless quantitative filter papers, Nylon/PTFE/PVDF membrane and syringe filters, and TLC chromatography plates and column chromatography silica gel.

B2B WORKFLOW

RFQ Inquiry Cart & WhatsApp Pricing

Built-in quote basket allowing procurement teams to aggregate chemical SKUs and dispatch instant RFQ inquiries via custom WhatsApp and email channels.

FIELD PROCUREMENT

Mobile-Optimized RFQ Journey

Laboratory managers and plant engineers often request chemical quotes directly from the lab bench or factory floor. The mobile experience was optimized to allow 1-tap chemical browsing and direct WhatsApp price confirmation.

  • Quick category switching between 5 chemical pillars
  • Instant WhatsApp quote dispatch with itemized lists
  • Direct telephone links to Bengaluru inventory desk
  • Fast rendering with no layout shift on mobile networks
